# Success stories

## Developing machine-learning tools through collaborative annotation studies

The primary determinant in machine-learning algorithm performance is the
availability of abundant labeled data for training. Collecting data for
applications such as tissue-region segmentation and cell classification is
challenging, given the limited availability of experts and of tools to collect
and review annotations.

The DSA web platform enables users to collaborate across the world and has been
used to generate more than 120,000 human markups of histopathology in multiple
annotation studies.

Its API and user roles allow collaborative annotation study teams that engage
users with different expertise levels, from pathologists to medical students,
to generate large and extensively reviewed annotation datasets. In one study
with more than 25 participants from over 5 countries, the team generated more
than 25,000 annotated tissue regions and produced the richest public dataset of
annotated breast-cancer tissues at the time. Programmatic monitoring and
management of these studies through the API is key to their success.

Data on this scale supports accurate machine-learning models for tasks such as
tissue segmentation, cell classification, and cell detection, and helps measure
the limits of concordance among human experts. The data from the collaborative
annotation study is available on a demo instance of the Digital Slide Archive.

## The Digital Slide Archive and TCGA

The Cancer Digital Slide Archive (CDSA) hosts whole-slide images of pathologic
specimens associated with The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) studies. At more than
10,000 images and 20 terabytes, the CDSA has enabled a number of TCGA Analysis
Working Group studies, as well as other investigator-driven studies that use
the digital pathology images.

The Digital Slide Archive builds on that experience, creating a software
platform that lets users run their own digital pathology servers for data
management and hosting in their own investigations.

[View the CDSA demo site](https://cancer.digitalslidearchive.org/){ .md-button }
